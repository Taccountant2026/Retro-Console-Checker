RETRO CONSOLE CHECKER — PRIVACY

Retro Console Checker is designed to work without creating an account.

My Collection

Console models saved to My Collection are stored locally in your browser/device using local storage. This information is not intentionally transmitted to Retro Console Checker by that feature.

Console searches

The core console-identification database runs in the app. No personal information is required to search or browse console records.

External links

The app may contain links to RetroReplay UK or reference sources. Those destinations may operate under their own privacy practices.

Device permissions

The current app does not require contacts, location, microphone, SMS or call-log permissions for its core functions.

Changes

If future versions add accounts, cloud synchronisation, analytics or other data processing, this notice and the relevant app-store disclosures must be updated before those features are released.
